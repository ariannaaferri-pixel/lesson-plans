Learning intention

Today we will be learning how Earth's surface and internal processes transform rocks from one type to another so we can use a model to explain how matter cycles through the rock cycle.

What we did: We modeled the rock cycle with crayons. We shaved, layered, pressed, and stomped them, then melted them into "igneous rock."
