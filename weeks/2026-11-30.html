Friday12/4
Learning intention

Today we will be learning how the positions of the Sun, Earth, and Moon decide what an observer sees so we can explain what Buffalo saw during the 2024 eclipse and what the Artemis II crew saw from the Moon.

What we did: We used data from the 2024 eclipse over Buffalo to compare total and partial eclipses, planned a trip to see the 2045 eclipse, and figured out how Earth looks from the Moon.

  1. Solve
  2. The Eclipse Over Buffalo and Earth From the MoonUse eclipse data and maps to explain what different places saw, then predict how Earth looks from the Moon.
