Learning intention

Today we will be learning how objects move in their orbits around the Sun so we can predict how fast an object travels and how long its year will be.

What we did: We drew orbits with pins and string, used Kepler's equal-area rule to compare speeds, and found the pattern between distance and the length of a year.
