Monday10/26
Learning intention

Today we will be learning how matter cycles through Earth's systems, using carbon as our example, so we can explain how carbon moves among the air, oceans, living things, and rocks, and why wetlands like the Everglades matter.

What we did: We did the Put It Together entry ticket from the Rock Cycle Infographic, then started the Cycles and System Models Learning Guide. We also got the Unit 2 Test Review Sheet.

Learning intention

Learning intention

Today we will be learning how a carbon atom can travel from a tree to limestone so we can trace carbon through Earth's spheres and explain which steps are part of the slow carbon cycle.
