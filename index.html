No class (Electives Expo)
Tuesday12/1
Learning intention

Today we will be learning how Earth's tilt and the Sun's changing path through the sky cause the seasons so we can explain why December 21 is the shortest day of the year in Buffalo.

What we did: We practiced Regents-style questions about the winter solstice, then completed our anchor chart on seasons and motions in the sky.

  1. Solve
  2. Cluster Practice: The Shortest DayUse the data table to answer questions about daylight and the noon Sun on the winter solstice.
    Due 12/2Open assignment
  3. Anchor Chart: Seasons and Motions in the SkyComplete the Seasons and Motions in the Sky anchor chart in your Unit 3 packet. The whole packet is due on the Unit 3 test day.
    Due 12/15Open assignment
Wednesday12/2
Learning intention

Today we will be learning how the positions of the Sun, Earth, and Moon cause the Moon's phases, eclipses, and tides so we can use evidence to explain and predict these repeating cycles.

What we did: We took the Earth-Sun System mastery check, then started the Earth-Moon System learning guide.

  1. Solve
  2. Quiz 7: Earth-Sun System Mastery CheckIf you were absent, you need to make this up in the AAC.
  3. Learning Guide: The Earth-Moon SystemRead about phases, eclipses, and tides and answer the Check for Understanding questions.
    Due 12/4Open assignment
Thursday12/3
Learning intention

Today we will be learning to spot mistakes about the Moon so we can use what causes phases, eclipses, and tides as evidence to correct them.

What we did: We finished the Earth-Moon System learning guide, watched the Artemis II flyby photos report for our video log, and fact-checked social media posts about the Moon.

  1. Solve
  2. Finish Learning Guide: The Earth-Moon SystemFinish the learning guide. It is due tomorrow.
    Due 12/4Open assignment
  3. Artemis II Video Log: Phase or FakeWatch the video and answer questions 15-18 in your video log. The whole log is due on the Unit 3 test day.
    Due 12/15Open assignment
    UsesWatch: Artemis II Flyby Photos News Report (2:41)
  4. Phase or Fake: Fact-Check the FeedWith a partner, decide if each post is real or fake and write a Community Note that fixes the fakes using evidence.
    Due 12/4Open assignment
Friday12/4
Learning intention

Today we will be learning how the positions of the Sun, Earth, and Moon decide what an observer sees so we can explain what Buffalo saw during the 2024 eclipse and what the Artemis II crew saw from the Moon.

What we did: We used data from the 2024 eclipse over Buffalo to compare total and partial eclipses, planned a trip to see the 2045 eclipse, and figured out how Earth looks from the Moon.

  1. Solve
  2. The Eclipse Over Buffalo and Earth From the MoonUse eclipse data and maps to explain what different places saw, then predict how Earth looks from the Moon.
    Due 12/7Open assignment
    UsesWatch: Buffalo Eclipse Time-Lapse (WGRZ)Watch: 99% vs. Totality Comparison (optional)Open: 2045 Eclipse Path MapOpen: 2045 Interactive Eclipse Map (zoom in on any city)Read: Earthset Photo (NASA)
