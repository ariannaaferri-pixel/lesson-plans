Monday10/5
Learning intention

Today we will be learning how Earth materials differ from place to place so we can explain that landscapes are made of matter that has been changed by energy. Today we will be learning how energy from the Sun and from Earth's interior moves through Earth's systems by conduction, convection, and radiation so we can explain what powers Yellowstone's hot springs, the winds over Yellowstone Lake, the slow movement of Earth's mantle, and why melting ice can lead to even more warming.
